1. Summary

At Supacms, we are committed to delivering a product you love. Because we want you to feel confident when upgrading, we back every new paid subscription with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are granted, how to request one, and what to expect after your request is submitted. We aim to make this process as simple and stress-free as possible.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe to any paid Supacms plan (Standard or PRO) and are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of the date your subscription began.

The 14-day window begins on the date your first payment was processed — not the date you first log in or begin using features.

✅ The 14-day guarantee applies to all new paid subscriptions — both Standard ($7/mo) and PRO ($18/mo) plans. Refunds are issued to the original payment method in full, with no deductions.

3. Refund Eligibility

A refund will be granted under the 14-day guarantee if all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your refund request is submitted within 14 calendar days of your initial payment
  • The request is submitted in writing to our support email (see Section 5)
  • You have not previously received a refund from Supacms under this or any prior guarantee
  • The request relates to a paid subscription (not a free plan)
  • Your account is in good standing and has not been terminated for policy violations

You do not need to provide a reason for your refund request within the 14-day window. We appreciate feedback but will never withhold a refund due to lack of explanation.

4. Non-Eligible Cases

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted more than 14 days after the initial payment date
  • Requests for recurring charges after the initial billing period (i.e., month 2, 3, and onwards)
  • Accounts terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy
  • Accounts where a refund has previously been granted
  • Requests citing technical issues that were not reported to support prior to the refund request
  • Requests where the account holder disputes a charge through their bank before contacting us

⚠️ After the 14-day window closes, subscription fees are non-refundable. If you cancel your subscription, your access to premium features will continue until the end of your paid billing period.

Requesting a refund is simple. Please follow these steps:

1
Email our support team
Send an email to nabeelsaleemshaikh@gmail.com with the subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Account Email]"
2
Include your account details
In the email body, include: your registered account email address, the plan you subscribed to (Standard or PRO), the approximate date of purchase, and optionally — any feedback on why you're requesting a refund.
3
Wait for confirmation
Our support team will confirm receipt of your request within 1–2 business days. We'll verify eligibility and begin processing your refund.
4
Receive your refund
Once approved, the refund will be issued to your original payment method. See Section 6 for expected timelines.

6. Refund Processing Time

Once your refund is approved, please allow the following timeframes for the funds to appear:

  • Internal processing: 1–2 business days for our team to review and approve your request
  • Payment processor: 3–5 business days for our Merchant of Record to issue the refund
  • Bank / card network: 3–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank

Total estimated time from request to funds: 5–14 business days. If you have not received your refund within 14 business days, please contact us again and we will investigate with our payment provider.

7. Cancellations

You can cancel your Supacms subscription at any time — no penalties, no exit fees, and no lengthy cancellation process. To cancel:

  • Log in to your Supacms dashboard
  • Navigate to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription
  • Confirm the cancellation

Upon cancellation:

  • Your subscription will not renew at the next billing date
  • Your access to all premium features will remain active until the end of your current billing period
  • Your uploaded content and account data will be retained for 90 days, after which it will be permanently deleted
  • You will not be charged any cancellation fees

Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund unless you are within the 14-day money-back guarantee window and submit a refund request as described in Section 5.

8. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. In most cases, we can resolve billing issues faster than the chargeback process allows.

Filing a fraudulent chargeback for a charge that was legitimate and for which we have provided the agreed service is a violation of our Terms of Service and may result in:

  • Immediate account suspension or permanent termination
  • Disqualification from future refund requests
  • Being banned from creating new Supacms accounts

If you believe a charge was unauthorised or fraudulent, please contact us immediately at nabeelsaleemshaikh@gmail.com before contacting your bank.

9. Annual Plan Refunds

If an annual billing option is offered by Supacms and you have subscribed on an annual basis:

  • The same 14-day money-back guarantee applies from the date of your first annual payment
  • After 14 days, annual plan payments are non-refundable in full or in part
  • If you cancel an annual plan after 14 days, you retain access until the end of the 12-month period but will not receive a prorated refund for unused months

We recommend starting with a monthly plan to trial the Service before committing to an annual subscription.

10. Exceptional Circumstances

We recognise that not all situations are black and white. In exceptional circumstances — such as prolonged platform downtime exceeding our 99.9% SLA, billing errors caused by a bug on our end, or duplicate charges — we will review refund requests on a case-by-case basis outside of the standard 14-day window.

Exceptional circumstance requests must:

  • Be submitted within 30 days of the issue occurring
  • Include a clear description of the issue and any supporting documentation
  • Be sent to nabeelsaleemshaikh@gmail.com with subject: "Exceptional Refund Request — [Your Account Email]"

We make no guarantee of approving exceptional requests, but we commit to reviewing them fairly and responding within 5 business days.
